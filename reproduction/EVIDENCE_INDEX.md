## New handover tools and review material

- tools/verify_handover.py: entire packet/component integrity and blank-review state checks.
- tools/reproduce_offline.py: fixed no-model verification/replay plan with logs and expected-byte comparisons.
- tools/audit_saved_rehearsal.py: portable fork of Task 12’s technical auditor. Only the absolute-source lookup is removed; included snapshots remain hash-checked, and every other check stays. The original auditor remains untouched; provenance/PORTABLE_AUDIT_CHANGE.json describes the exact change.
- tools/serve_readonly_gui.py: process-only wrapper serving the existing v2.5 views with an explicit banner/badge and generation blocked before worker creation. It is a verification helper, not a new production GUI release or live-answer fallback.
- tools/prepare_return.py, validate_review_return.py, seal_return.py: create blank forms, verify identities/schema and seal a separate return. They cannot supply human correctness judgments or Suresh’s acceptance.
- review/TASK12_REVIEW_PACKET.md and REVIEW_RECORDS.json: complete saved contexts and 20 unique response records, with exact hashes/survivor numbers/raw byte links. New forms remain pending.
- validation/: only checks actually performed for this delivery. Historical saved logs in components retain their own dates and scope.

The original reference study and the 127 completed teammate prefix remain attributed to their original sources. Task-1/2/8 comparison records and panels expose their coverage and text; this package does not pretend that the final recovery directory alone contains all 706 original raw network responses. Large original inputs/index/model layers remain in the already available original package/cache.
