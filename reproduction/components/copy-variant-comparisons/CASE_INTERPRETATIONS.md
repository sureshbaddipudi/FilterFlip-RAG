The frozen question asks which constitutional provision stated the compromise: Article 1, Section 2, Clause 3. PN1 and PE1 lose the designated complete quote but retain doc101561, which explicitly links that provision to the Three-Fifths Compromise. Both filtered answer panels give the requested provision. This is a concrete false alarm for treating complete-quote loss as sufficient-support loss. The one-copy conditions are already semantically supported through an alternative passage, so quote restoration in the two-copy arm does not by itself imply a new answer-quality improvement.
